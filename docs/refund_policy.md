# Refund Policy — LexTech

## Standard Refund Policy
LexTech offers a 14-day money-back guarantee for all new Pro Plan subscriptions.
If you are not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact support@lextech.com 
for a full refund. No questions asked.

## What is NOT refundable
The following are not eligible for refunds:
- Subscriptions older than 14 days
- Enterprise plan setup fees
- Add-on purchases such as extra storage
- Accounts that violated LexTech Terms of Service

## How to Request a Refund
1. Email support@lextech.com with subject line "Refund Request"
2. Include your account email and order number
3. Explain the reason for your refund request
4. Our team will process it within 5 business days
5. Refunds appear on your statement within 7-10 business days

## Refunds for Annual Plans
If you purchased an annual plan:
- Within 14 days: Full refund
- Between 14 and 30 days: 50% refund
- After 30 days: No refund, but you keep access until end of year

## Disputed Charges
If you see a charge you do not recognize, contact support@lextech.com 
immediately. We will investigate and respond within 48 hours.
