# Frequently Asked Questions — LexTech Support

## What is LexTech?
LexTech is a cloud-based project management software that helps teams 
collaborate, track tasks, and manage deadlines in real time.

## How do I create an account?
To create a LexTech account:
1. Go to www.lextech.com/signup
2. Enter your work email address
3. Choose a password that is at least 8 characters long
4. Click the "Create Account" button
5. Check your email for a verification link and click it

## How much does LexTech cost?
LexTech offers three pricing plans:
- Free Plan: Up to 3 users, 5 projects, 1GB storage
- Pro Plan: $12 per user per month, unlimited projects, 50GB storage
- Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing, unlimited everything, dedicated support

## Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your Account Settings 
page. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. 
No refunds are given for partial months.

## Does LexTech work on mobile?
Yes. LexTech has mobile apps for both iOS and Android. You can download them 
from the App Store or Google Play Store. All features available on desktop 
are also available on mobile.

## How do I contact support?
You can contact LexTech support in three ways:
- Live chat: Available 9am to 6pm EST Monday through Friday
- Email: support@lextech.com (response within 24 hours)
- Phone: 1-800-LEX-TECH (Enterprise plan customers only)
