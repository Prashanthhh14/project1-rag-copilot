# Security Policy — LexTech

## Password Requirements
All LexTech passwords must meet these requirements:
- Minimum 8 characters long
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character such as ! @ # $
- Cannot be the same as your last 5 passwords

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
LexTech strongly recommends enabling 2FA on all accounts.
Enterprise plan accounts have mandatory 2FA enforced by default.

To enable 2FA:
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Click Security
3. Click Enable Two-Factor Authentication
4. Choose SMS or Authenticator App
5. Follow the setup steps

## Data Encryption
All data stored in LexTech is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
All data transmitted between your browser and LexTech is encrypted using TLS 1.3.

## Session Management
- Sessions automatically expire after 30 days of inactivity
- You can log out all devices from Account Settings → Security → Active Sessions
- If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and 
  contact security@lextech.com

## Reporting Security Issues
If you discover a security vulnerability in LexTech:
- Email security@lextech.com immediately
- Do not share the vulnerability publicly before we fix it
- We will respond within 24 hours and credit you in our security hall of fame
