# New User Onboarding Guide — LexTech

## Welcome to LexTech!
This guide will help you get started in your first 30 minutes.

## Step 1: Set Up Your Profile (5 minutes)
1. Click your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select "Edit Profile"
3. Add your full name, job title, and profile photo
4. Set your timezone so deadlines show correctly for you
5. Click Save

## Step 2: Create Your First Project (10 minutes)
1. Click the blue "New Project" button on your dashboard
2. Enter a project name and description
3. Choose a project color for easy identification
4. Set the project start date and deadline
5. Click "Create Project"

## Step 3: Invite Your Team (5 minutes)
1. Open your project
2. Click "Members" in the left sidebar
3. Click "Invite Member"
4. Enter your teammate's email address
5. Choose their role: Viewer, Editor, or Admin
6. Click Send Invite
Your teammate will receive an email invitation within 5 minutes.

## Step 4: Create Your First Task (10 minutes)
1. Inside your project, click "Add Task"
2. Enter the task name
3. Assign it to a team member
4. Set a due date
5. Add a priority level: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
6. Click Save

## User Roles Explained
- Viewer: Can see tasks but cannot edit anything
- Editor: Can create and edit tasks but cannot manage members
- Admin: Full access including member management and project settings
- Owner: The person who created the project, has all permissions

## Getting Help
If you get stuck at any point:
- Click the "?" icon in the bottom right corner for instant help
- Visit our help center at help.lextech.com
- Email support@lextech.com
